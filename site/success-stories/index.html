Choosing the right estate agent is key in today’s market

Is your home not selling? When a property sits on the market with little interest, it is rarely a problem with the property itself—it is usually a matter of presentation and strategy. The right estate agent can be invaluable in making your property sale a success story, offering guidance and advice. That was exactly the case for a couple of homeowners whose property sale in Beverley seemed to have ground to a alt. They came to us after three frustrating months with another high street agent. Despite being a wonderful home in a central area of Beverley, the property had received just one viewing in ninety days. Unsure of what to do next, the sellers reached out to our team for a fresh perspective.

Our Strategy

Instead of simply lowering the price, we sat down with the owners to look at the bigger picture:

  • Tailored Staging Advice: We provided actionable guidance on kerb appeal, decluttering, adjusting furniture layouts, and maximizing natural light to highlight the home’s best features making it more appealing.
  • Professional Refresh: Once the recommendations were in place, we captured fresh, high-quality photography and crafted a listing description that truly told the story of the home.
  • Targeted Marketing: We launched a refreshed campaign focused on reaching active buyers specifically searching in the Beverley market.

The Result

The response was immediate:

By making small, strategic adjustments to presentation and marketing, we transformed a stagnant listing into a highly competitive property.

A sensitive transition handled with care in Swanland

  • The Property: A spacious 5-bedroom detached family home featuring a generous garden, situated in a highly sought-after Swanland village location near excellent schooling.
  • The Challenge: Selling a home during a personal separation is always emotionally complex. Adding to the challenge, the property required a little cosmetic TLC, and we launched the listing in the depths of winter—traditionally a quieter period for the property market.
  • Our Approach: Sincerity and trust are at the heart of what we do. We had an open, honest conversation with the clients regarding current market conditions and realistic value. We then provided practical guidance on staging and minor improvements to ensure the home’s space and potential shone through during viewings.
  • The Result: Our targeted property marketing struck the right chord. Within just a few weeks, we secured multiple viewings and a fantastic offer. By proactively managing the conveyancing process, we ensured a smooth path to completion, allowing both clients to confidently close this chapter and move forward with their separate, exciting future plans.
