Welcome to The Woolpack

Good food, good ale, and room to breathe

Set on the edge of the Beverley Westwood pastures, The Woolpack is a traditional pub for a slow lunch, a Sunday roast or a quiet pint after a walk.

Dine in or grab a seat outside, and bring the dog along. Reservations are available, so give us a call if you want to be sure of a table.
