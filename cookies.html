Cookies

What we store on your device, why, and how to control it.

Strictly necessary

These keep the site working. They don't need your consent.

  • Website builder progress: your business name, type and location are kept in your browser's session storage while you use the builder. It's deleted when you close the tab.
  • Account sign-in: if you create an account, your login is kept in local storage so you stay signed in.
  • Payments (Stripe): the checkout pages set cookies such as __stripe_mid and __stripe_sid to prevent fraud. See Stripe's cookie policy.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts: pages load fonts from Google, which is needed to display the site. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Vercel Web Analytics: counts page views without setting cookies on your device. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Not used

We don't use advertising cookies or tracking cookies, and we don't follow you across other websites.

Controlling cookies

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop sign-in and checkout from working. Questions? Email brightsiteapp@gmail.com.
