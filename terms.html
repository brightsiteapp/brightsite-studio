Terms & conditions

1. Who we are

BrightSite ("we", "us") builds and manages websites for brands and small businesses. You can reach us at brightsiteapp@gmail.com or on WhatsApp. These terms apply to everything we build or manage for you, and by asking us to start work you accept them.

2. What we agree to build

Before any work starts we'll confirm in writing what's included — the pages, the features and the price. Anything outside that is extra work, and we'll agree it with you before doing it rather than adding it to a bill afterwards.

3. Prices and payment

One-off builds are quoted as a fixed price and are payable on completion, before the site goes live. Management plans are billed monthly in advance and roll month to month.

Prices shown on our pricing page apply to new work. If we change them, existing management plans keep their agreed price for at least 30 days, and we'll tell you before any change takes effect.

4. What we need from you

To hit any timeline we need your content — text, images, logos, opening hours, prices — and reasonably prompt answers to questions. If we're waiting on you, timelines move.

You confirm that anything you send us is yours to use, or that you have permission to use it. That matters most for photography: images taken from Google, Instagram or another salon's website are not yours to publish, and we can't put them on your site.

5. Ownership

Once a one-off build is paid for in full, the design and the code of your website are yours. Your content, brand and photographs are yours throughout and always were. Any third-party components — fonts, stock imagery, plugins — stay under their own licences.

We'd like to show your site in our portfolio and link to it. If you'd rather we didn't, just say and we won't.

6. Management plans

Management covers ongoing changes, updates, SEO and the other items listed for your plan. "Same-day changes" means changes we receive on a working day, and covers ordinary edits — text, prices, images, hours, offers — not a redesign or a new section, which we'd quote separately.

Support is available at any time and we answer as quickly as we can, but we're a small team and not a 24-hour staffed helpdesk. Urgent means urgent: if your site is down, tell us and it goes to the front of the queue.

7. Cancelling

Management plans are rolling monthly. Cancel any time by telling us before your next billing date — no notice period and no exit fee. We don't refund part-months, and cover stops at the end of the month you've paid for.

If you cancel management, your website stays yours. We'll help you move it to your own hosting, and we'll hand over what you need to run it.

8. Hosting, domains and things we don't control

Where we host or manage a domain on your behalf, we'll tell you what it costs and who the provider is. Parts of your site depend on services we don't run — hosting providers, domain registrars, booking tools, Google, social platforms — and we're not responsible for their outages or for changes they make to their own products.

9. SEO

We build sites to be found, and we work on your ranking continuously. Nobody can guarantee a position in Google's results, and we don't. If anyone promises you page one, be careful.

10. Liability

We'll do our work with reasonable care and skill. If something goes wrong and it's our fault, our liability is limited to the amount you've paid us in the previous twelve months. We're not liable for lost profits or lost business. Nothing here limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that can't be limited by law.

11. Governing law

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have jurisdiction.

Privacy notice

What we collect

If you contact us through our website, we collect what you send: your name, email address, and business details. If you call or email directly, we collect the same information. If you become a client we also hold the details needed to do the work and to invoice you. We only collect what we need to respond to your enquiry or deliver our service.

Why we hold it, and for how long

We use your details to answer your enquiry, to carry out work you've asked for, and to send information about that work. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone except the suppliers needed to run the service — for example our hosting provider (Vercel), email service, or payment processor. Enquiries that don't turn into work are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for six years, which is what UK tax law requires for accounting records.

Contact form processing

When you submit our contact form, your information is sent to BrightSite via a secure server. We use it only to get back to you about your preview website or services. By submitting the form you confirm you've read and accept our privacy notice.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email brightsiteapp@gmail.com and we'll deal with it within a month. If you think we've handled your data badly, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use any technology to follow you across other websites. Under the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), we are required to tell you what we do set:

  • Strictly necessary session storage: When you use the website builder on our homepage, your progress (business name, type, location) is kept temporarily in your browser's session storage so the page works correctly. This is deleted when you close the tab and is never sent to our servers except as part of the normal form submission.
  • Account session (if you log in): If you create an account, we store your login token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. This is strictly necessary to provide the account service and does not require your consent.
  • Google Fonts: Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which causes your browser to make a request to Google's servers. This is a technical necessity to render the page and is not used for tracking or advertising. Google's privacy policy applies to that request.

We do not use any analytics tools, advertising networks, or third-party tracking scripts on this website. No consent banner is therefore required under PECR for this site. Sites we build for clients may use analytics or booking cookies — where they do, those sites carry their own cookie notice.

Minimum age and children

Our service is intended for business owners and is not directed at children. You must be at least 18 years old to enter into a contract with us or to create an account. If you are under 18, please do not submit your details through our website.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that we have collected data from someone under 18, we will delete it promptly. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us at brightsiteapp@gmail.com.

These provisions are in accordance with the UK GDPR, the Age Appropriate Design Code (the Children's Code) issued by the Information Commissioner's Office, and the general requirement under the UK GDPR that the processing of children's personal data requires a higher standard of protection.
